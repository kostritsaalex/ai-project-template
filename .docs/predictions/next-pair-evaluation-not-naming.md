# Next pair: evaluation rather than naming

**Registered 2026-09-05, before any next pair is designed or a subject is chosen.**

## Not yet run, and this is disclosed rather than deferred quietly

No pair has been run against these predictions. This file exists so that when one is, the
expectation is on record beforehand rather than reconstructed after. Its verdict, when it
exists, will be in the decision record that cites it and in [`../runs/`](../runs/README.md).

The design any such pair must satisfy is
[`0028`](../decisions/0028-an-arm-pair-differs-by-one-variable.md). These predictions do not
replace that; they say what to expect once it is met.

## What one pair has already shown, so that these are not confused with it

One pair ran on 2026-09-05, on a WordPress subject, arms differing by two insertions with
disjoint targets. Its three registered predictions held: the treatment named an alternative the
control did not, the treatment named a revisit condition the control did not, and both arms
reached the same decision.

**Its most useful result was not one of them.** The difference between the arms was mostly not
which alternatives were named. It was whether they were evaluated. That is a post-hoc reading
of that pair and it is the reason these predictions exist.

Measured there, from the records rather than from the judge's discussion: treatment four
alternatives in its options section, three carrying explicit buys and fails, the fourth declared
not live by the record itself; control two alternatives named in prose, none evaluated.

## The predictions

**P1. Evaluation, not naming.** Both arms name comparable alternatives. The treatment
evaluates more of them: for each named alternative it states what that option buys and how it
fails, where the control names without evaluating. The quantity is the count of named
alternatives that carry both halves, judged from each record's own text.

**P2. The shared sections contract.** Under the parts under test, the sections both arms share
come out shorter in the treatment while whole-document length holds roughly constant.

Measured in the one pair, headings excluded: Context 365 against 461, Decision 99 against 108,
Consequences without the revisit block 421 against 612, Origin 68 against 143. Shared total 953
against 1324, a difference of minus 371, against 451 words of new sections. Whole documents 1468
against 1367.

**Shorter in all four is one observation and not four**, since a drafter working to a length
budget shortens everything at once. Falsifiable on the next pair with no new instrument.

**P3. Redirection rather than addition.** The parts under test move evaluative effort from the
chosen option onto the rejected ones rather than adding it. In the one pair the control's
Consequences ran 612 words, the largest section in either document, against 421 in the
treatment.

**Word counts cannot test this.** A shorter section is equally consistent with content moved
elsewhere and with the same content written more tightly. **P3 requires a judge question about
consequence CONTENT**: what does each record establish about the cost, the risks accepted and
the recovery path, and does either omit something the other supplies. Without that question P3
is not tested by a pair that measures length.

## The watch item this pair should also close

**The revisit trigger's wording may invite a decorative date.** The one treatment record
reasoned three revisit conditions from its material and then gave a scheduled date one year
from the drafting day, derived from nothing.

- **Closes as unfounded** if two or three further records under the current wording produce
  dates deriving from something real: a contract term, an end of life, a season.
- **Closes as confirmed**, and the part changes to prefer a condition and admit a date only
  where derived, if two or three further records produce dates deriving from nothing.

Either close ends the item. It is written with both because a watch item with only a dismissal
condition is a permanent doubt rather than a pending question.

## What this cannot settle

A pair against these predictions does not close
[`0024`](../decisions/0024-a-decision-record-names-its-options-and-its-revisit-trigger.md)'s
condition for shipping the form into `blueprints/` unless its subject comes from outside the
domain the first pair came from. One domain measured twice is one domain.
